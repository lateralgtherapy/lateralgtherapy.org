Why the name

Commit to the corner

Lateral G is the sideways force you feel in a hard corner, the moment the car loads up and you have to commit. Founder Peter Gerbracht, a 20-year Air National Guard veteran and a survivor of suicide loss, found that professional help, people who understood, and time behind the wheel got him through. Lateral G Therapy exists so others don't have to find those things alone.
