Find help

Help is real, and it's here

You don't have to have the right words or be at your worst to reach out. Every resource here is free or can point you to low-cost care. If one door doesn't fit, try the next.

Talk to someone now

Free, confidential, 24/7. You can call for yourself or for someone you're worried about. If anyone is in immediate danger, call 911.

Call or text

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

988

Call or text 988, or chat online. Trained counselors listen and help, whatever you're going through.

Veterans & service members

Veterans Crisis Line

988, press 1

Or text 838255. Many responders are veterans themselves. You don't need to be enrolled in VA care.

HOME to 741741

A quick note

Lateral G Therapy is a peer support community, not a crisis service and not therapy. We don't provide treatment or counseling. We point you to people who do. If you're thinking about suicide, please reach out to 988 now; you deserve support today.

If you're in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, free, 24/7). Veterans: dial 988 then press 1, or text 838255. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911.
