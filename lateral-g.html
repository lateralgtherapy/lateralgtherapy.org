Lateral G

Motorsports as a place to focus, breathe, and find your people. Not instead of professional help, but alongside it.

What it is

Behind the wheel, there's only room for the next corner. Lateral G brings men, veterans, and survivors of suicide loss together at real motorsports events, where the focus of driving and the welcome of the paddock can do a lot of good.

You don't need a race car, a fast car, or any experience. Come to watch, ride along, help work the course, or drive your own car. Showing up is enough.

What we're planning

  • Paddock meetups at local autocross and track-day events, run with established clubs like SCCA regions.
  • Ride-alongs with experienced drivers where events allow them.
  • First-timer help: walking the course, tech inspection, and what to expect.
  • Conversation, not pressure. Talk if you want to. Just hang out if you don't.

Safety first, always

Motorsports are only an outlet when they're done safely. Every Lateral G activity follows these ground rules.

  • Driving happens only at organized, sanctioned events, with the host club's safety rules, insurance, and waivers. Never on public roads.
  • Helmets and the event's tech inspection are required for anyone on course.
  • No alcohol or drugs before or during driving. Sober paddock, every time.
  • It's always okay to sit a session out. If you're not in a good place to drive, hang out with us instead.
  • Lateral G is peer support, not therapy. If someone is in crisis at an event, we connect them with 988 or emergency services right away.

If you're in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, free, 24/7). Veterans: dial 988 then press 1, or text 838255. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911.
